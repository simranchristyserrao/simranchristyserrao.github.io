About Me

I am currently pursuing my Master’s Thesis titled “An Analysis of Social Network Dynamics among Indian STEM Undergraduates: Implications for Targeted Empathy Interventions” at BITS Pilani, KK Birla Goa Campus as a dual major in Computer Science and Biological Sciences.

My research interests lie in Social Psychology—I use Natural Language Processing to study social media posts within small-world networks and design targeted empathy interventions. My long-term goal is to help develop empathy interventions that promote prosocial behavior among university students.

In my free time, I always look forward to a cup of coffee and a quiet nature walk. I am passionate about Digital art and work part-time as an illustrator for medical education.
